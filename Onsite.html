In person support offerings

Appalachian IT Support brings trusted on-site service directly to your home. Our technicians arrive prepared to troubleshoot personal computers, laptops, printers, wireless routers, and smart devices with friendly, face-to-face support.

Whether you need a system tune-up, hardware repair, virus removal, or help setting up a new home network, our goal is to make technology easy and reliable for you. We work carefully to minimize downtime and explain each step in plain language.

What to expect from our on-site visits

Each visit starts with a clear assessment of the issue, followed by a recommended solution and transparent pricing. We handle repairs, upgrades, and installations for desktops, laptops, printers, Wi-Fi systems, security cameras, and smart home devices.

  • Fast diagnosis and repair for PCs, laptops, and peripherals.
  • Home networking, Wi-Fi optimization, and troubleshooting.
  • Printer setup, scanner integration, and device connectivity.
  • Security camera installation and smart home device support.
  • Clear communication, follow-up advice, and satisfaction guarantee.
